Privacy Policy

Home Storage · Effective November 7, 2026

Home Storage is provided by Ha Thi Nguyen. This policy describes version 1.0.0 of the iOS app.

Your inventory stays on your device

The app stores item names, photos, locations, notes, tags, loan records and moving checklists locally. It does not require an account, operate an inventory server, or transmit this inventory to the developer. The app has no advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs.

Permissions

Camera access is used only when you take a photo or scan a box label. The system photo picker gives the app access to the photos you select. Notification permission is used for optional local loan reminders. You can change permissions in iOS Settings.

Backups and sharing

You can export inventory backups and share box labels using system services. A full backup includes photos and inventory details. Any service you choose for sharing or storage, and any iOS device backup you enable, operates under that service's own privacy policy. The developer does not receive these exports automatically.

Deletion

You can edit or delete your records in the app. Deleted items are retained in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days unless you permanently delete them sooner. Previously exported backups remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted separately. Removing the app deletes its local data; copies in backups may remain.

Support and this website

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send and uses it to respond to your request. Do not send sensitive inventory details unless necessary. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical information when you visit it under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact Ha Thi Nguyen at giapthithuhien8177@icloud.com. Material changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
