Home Storage

Everything has its place. Keep a photo inventory, organize rooms and boxes, and plan your move.

Support

Contact Ha Thi Nguyen at giapthithuhien8177@icloud.com. Include your iOS version, app version and a description of the issue. Please do not send passwords or private inventory photos.

Getting started

Add an item from Home or Items, give it a name and choose its location. Create rooms, cabinets, drawers and boxes in Spaces. Search Items to find your records again.

Box QR labels

A QR label identifies a box in your local inventory. The box record must exist on the scanning device. To use it on another device, first restore a full backup of the same inventory.

Backup and recovery

Export a full backup from Settings and keep it in a safe location. Backups include your inventory and photos. Review the restore confirmation before importing a backup. Deleted items are recoverable for up to 30 days unless permanently deleted.

Permissions

Camera access is optional and used for photos and QR scanning. Notifications are optional and used for loan reminders. The app interface is English only.
